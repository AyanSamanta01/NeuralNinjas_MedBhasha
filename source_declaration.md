## 3. Preprocessing Pipeline

Every clinical narrative underwent systematic text normalization and preprocessing preserving Bengali clinical semantics:
1. **Bengali Text Normalization:** Standardized Unicode character encodings, removed orphan diacritics and stray punctuation, and normalized zero-width joiners/non-joiners (ZWJ/ZWNJ).
2. **Medical Terminology Preservation:** Preserved composite transliterated clinical terms (e.g., 'ডায়াবেটিস মেলাইটাস', 'মায়োকার্ডিয়াল ইনফার্কশন', 'কনসোলিডেশন') and bilingual abbreviations ('সিটি স্ক্যান', 'এমআরআই', 'ইসিজি', 'ইউএসজি').
3. **Tokenization:** Tokenized into word tokens and clinical punctuation boundaries (`।`, `,`, `-`, `/`), guaranteeing that numeric dosages (e.g., `৫০০`, `মিগ্রা`) and ratio findings (e.g., `১৪০/৯০`) retain exact span alignment.
4. **Sentence Segmentation:** Multi-sentence clinical narratives structured sequentially with native Bengali terminators (`।`).

## 4. Annotation Methodology & Tagging Scheme

Annotations were conducted following the official `entity_schema.md` and `annotation_guidelines.pdf`:
- **Tagging Format:** Strict standard `BIO` (Beginning, Inside, Outside).
- **Entity Classes Tagged (10 Categories):**
  1. `DIAGNOSIS`: Confirmed/suspected clinical conditions (e.g., ডায়াবেটিস, নিউমোনিয়া).
  2. `SYMPTOM`: Complaints and clinical signs (e.g., জ্বর, কাশি, বুকে ব্যথা).
  3. `MEDICATION`: Brand and generic pharmaceuticals (e.g., মেটফরমিন, প্যারাসিটামল).
  4. `DOSAGE`: Quantitative strengths, frequencies, and routes (e.g., ৫০০ মিগ্রা, দিনে দুইবার).
  5. `TEST`: Named laboratory investigations and imaging (e.g., ইসিজি, রক্তচাপ পরীক্ষা).
  6. `TEST_RESULT`: Quantitative values and qualitative outcomes (e.g., ১৪০/৯০, পজিটিভ).
  7. `BODY_SITE`: Anatomical structures and organs (e.g., বুক, ফুসফুস, কিডনি).
  8. `PROCEDURE`: Interventional and surgical acts (e.g., এন্ডোস্কপি, অস্ত্রোপচার, ক্যানুলা).
  9. `TEMPORAL`: Durations, onsets, and follow-up expressions (e.g., গত তিন দিন ধরে).
  10. `NEGATION_UNCERTAINTY`: Negation markers and diagnostic uncertainty cues (e.g., নেই, অস্বীকার করে, সন্দেহ).
